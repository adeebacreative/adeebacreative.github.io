A little about me

who's behind the screen?

Hi! I'm Adeeba :-) I'm a Sydney-based designer with a background across varying creative and strategic fields. I believe in 改善 'kaizen', a Japanese word meaning continuous improvement. Basically, I'm on a lifelong mission to make today's ideas vastly cooler than yesterday's. I'm constantly upskilling myself and evolving my design frameworks... and my bubble tea order.

I have a Bachelors in IT and Business, and an MBA in Marketing. My history in design roots from passion and years of self learning and volunteering.

My interests are movies, art, video games, pop culture, fashion and tech (what's the fun in only having one, right?)
